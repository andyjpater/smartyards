# 🗓️ COACHING CALENDAR
Everything live happens here:
• **Weekly Sales Clinic** — group coaching on real deals
• **Workshops**
• **Guest sessions**
• **Special training**
• **Events**

**How to join:** click **Events** at the top of the channel list and hit **Interested**. Discord will notify you when it starts.

Want a reminder before each clinic? Pick the **Clinic Reminders** option in *Channels & Roles* at the top of the channel list.

**Sales Clinic format:** no lectures. Members bring current deals, objections, lost quotes, difficult customers, pricing situations and follow-up problems. We work through them live.

Want something covered? Drop it in {#submitAQuestion} beforehand.
