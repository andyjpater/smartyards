Got hit with an objection? Post the actual situation, and the Society helps you work out what to say.

Include:
• The objection, word for word
• What they’re buying and roughly what it’s worth
• What happened just before they said it
• What you said back (be honest)
• Where it’s at now

Replying? Write the actual words you’d use, not “build more value”. The best answers get turned into permanent resources in {#objectionPlaybook}.
