Submit sales messages, emails, SMS, phone scripts, proposals, quote presentations, call transcripts or sales videos for critique.

Include:
• What it is and where it’s used
• Who receives it (type of customer, stage of the sale)
• What you want it to achieve
• What’s happening now (reply rate, close rate, feedback)

Remove customer names, numbers and addresses before posting.

Reviewing? Be direct, be specific, and rewrite the line rather than just criticising it.
