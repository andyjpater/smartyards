# This is the most important channel in the Society.
Members bring **live deals** they’re trying to close. Coaches and members tell them exactly what to do **next**.

**Posting a deal**
1. Hit **New Post**
2. Title it with the job and value, e.g. *“$24k deck + pergola — quoted, gone quiet after 6 days”*
3. Paste the template (it’s in the post guidelines) and fill in every line
4. Tag the stage

**Replying to a deal**
✅ “I’d text her today: ‘Hi Sarah, had another look at your plans…’ then ask for a 10-min call Thursday.”
❌ “You need to build more value.”

Specific words, specific next step, specific timeframe.

**After**
Come back and update your post with what happened. Won it? Change the tag to **Won 🏆** and post the story in {#wins}. Lost it? Take it to {#lostDeals}. Either way, everyone learns.

🔒 *Strip customer names, numbers and addresses before posting.*
