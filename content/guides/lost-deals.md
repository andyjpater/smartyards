# Lost deals are the best teachers in the room.
Everyone loses jobs. The businesses that grow are the ones who work out *why*.

The point of this channel isn’t embarrassment. It’s building a big library of real Aussie sales lessons so nobody has to lose the same deal twice.

**Post it:**
```
Deal value:
What happened:
Where I think I lost them:
What they told me:
What I’d do differently:
```
Tag the reason you think you lost it.

**Replying:**
• Find the **moment** it was lost (usually earlier than people think)
• Say what you’d have done at that moment
• Be honest, not brutal

Plenty of “lost” deals come back with the right follow-up. Check {#followUpPlaybook} before you write it off.
