# Objections are just unanswered questions.
Post the objection you got hit with and the Society will help you work out what to say.

**Title it with the objection word for word**, e.g. *“I’ve got a cheaper quote from another bloke” — $9k switchboard upgrade*

Then give context: what they’re buying, what happened just before, what you said back, where it’s at now.

**Good replies look like this:**
> “Totally fair to compare. Can I ask — is the other quote for the same scope? A lot of people find the difference is in what’s NOT included. Mind if we go through it line by line?”

Actual words, not theory.

Common ones already covered in {#objectionPlaybook}: too expensive · cheaper quote · need to think about it · talk to my partner · just email it · getting three quotes · not interested · we’ll call you · can you do it cheaper.

The best answers from here get added to the playbook.
