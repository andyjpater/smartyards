# Get your sales messages torn apart (nicely).
Post an email, SMS, script, proposal, quote, call recording or video and get specific feedback.

**Title it with what it is and the goal**, e.g. *“Follow-up SMS after site visit — trying to get replies”*

**Tell us:**
• Who gets it and at what stage
• What it’s meant to achieve
• How it’s going now

**Reviewers — the standard:**
1. What’s working (one line)
2. The biggest thing to fix
3. A rewritten version they can copy

No vague “make it punchier”. Rewrite the line.

🔒 *Blur customer names, numbers, emails and addresses before posting.*
