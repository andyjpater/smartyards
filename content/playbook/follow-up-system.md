# 📨 THE FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM
Most jobs aren’t lost to competitors. They’re lost to silence.

This is the follow-up system for every stage of a trade/service sale. Work down the list. Each stage: **when**, **how**, and **what to say**. Grab the full wording in {#scriptsAndTemplates}.

**The rules**
• Every contact ends with a next step and a date
• Mix channels: call → SMS → email
• Each follow-up adds something new (an idea, an answer, a date) — never just “checking in”
• You own the follow-up. Never leave it to the customer
<!-- split -->
**1️⃣ NEW ENQUIRY**
⏱ Within 5 minutes. 📞 Call first.
Goal: book a conversation or site visit. The first business to speak to them usually wins.

**2️⃣ MISSED CALL**
⏱ Immediately after. 💬 SMS.
> “Hi [Name], [You] from [Business]. Just tried calling about your [job]. When’s a good time for a quick chat?”
Call again same day, different time.

**3️⃣ SITE VISIT**
⏱ Before you leave. 🤝 In person.
Book the quote walkthrough on the spot. Same day: SMS thanking them and confirming when the quote is coming.

**4️⃣ QUOTE SENT**
⏱ When you send it. 📞 Walkthrough call booked.
Never send a quote without a scheduled conversation to go through it.
<!-- split -->
**5️⃣ 24-HOUR FOLLOW-UP**
⏱ Next day. 💬 SMS or 📞 call.
> “Just checking the quote came through OK and made sense. Any questions?”

**6️⃣ SEVERAL-DAY FOLLOW-UP**
⏱ Day 3–5. 📞 Call, then SMS if no answer.
Add value: an idea, an answer to a question they hadn’t asked, a timing option. Ask directly: “Where are you at with it?”

**7️⃣ GHOSTED QUOTE**
⏱ Day 7–14. ✉️ Email + 💬 SMS.
The **1, 2 or 3** email (in {#scriptsAndTemplates}), or:
> “Should I hold a spot for your job in [month], or let it go for now? Either is fine.”
Giving permission to say no gets replies.
<!-- split -->
**8️⃣ LONG-TERM FOLLOW-UP**
⏱ Monthly, then quarterly. 💬 SMS / ✉️ email.
Not ready ≠ not buying. Stay useful and visible: a seasonal tip, a relevant job you just finished, availability updates.

**9️⃣ LOST OPPORTUNITY**
⏱ When they say no. 📞 Call if you can.
> “Thanks for letting me know. Can I ask what tipped it? It helps us get better.”
Log the reason. Post it in {#lostDeals}. Check back in 3–6 months. Other trades let people down more than you’d think.

**🔟 OLD DATABASE REACTIVATION**
⏱ Twice a year minimum. 💬 SMS batches of 20–50.
Old quotes and past customers: “We quoted your [job] back in [month]. Did that ever get sorted?”
Then **call everyone who replies**. This is the cheapest revenue in your business.
