# 📚 RESOURCES
Share useful books, videos, podcasts, software, tools and sales resources.

**Rules for posting**
• Say **why** it’s useful and who it’s for, one or two lines
• No affiliate links, no promoting your own products or services
• If you use a tool in your business (CRM, quoting software, call tracking), tell us what you like and hate about it. That’s gold.

Posts that are just a link with no context get removed.
