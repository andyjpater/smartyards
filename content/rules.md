## 📋 THE RULES
Short list. We actually enforce it.

**1. Bring real problems.**
The more context you give, the better advice you get. “How do I close more?” gets a shrug. “$18k bathroom reno, quoted Tuesday, she said she needs to talk to her husband” gets a game plan.

**2. No ego.**
Lost deals are often worth more than won deals. Nobody here is going to laugh at you. We’ve all stuffed up quotes.

**3. Give before you take.**
If you can help another member, help them. The best way to get good at sales is to coach someone else through a deal.

**4. Protect customer information.**
Strip out customer names, phone numbers, emails and addresses before you post screenshots, quotes or conversations. “Mrs S in Penrith” is plenty.

**5. No unsolicited pitching or DMs.**
This is a sales community, not your prospect list. Don’t cold-DM members, don’t drop referral links, don’t pitch your services. Instant removal.

**6. Implementation beats information.**
The goal isn’t to learn more about sales. It’s to sell more. Try the thing, then come back and tell us what happened.

Break the rules and a Coach will have a word. Break them twice and you’re out. See something dodgy? Right-click the message → **Apps → Report to Coaches**.
