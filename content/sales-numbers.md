# 🔢 SALES NUMBERS
The maths behind selling more. Discuss, compare, ask.

• Close rate
• Lead conversion (lead → appointment)
• Quote conversion (quote → win)
• Average transaction value
• Revenue per lead
• Pipeline value
• Follow-up rates
• Sales cycle length
• Lost quote reasons
• Sales capacity (how many quotes can your team actually handle?)

**Why it matters:** 40 leads a month × 50% quoted × 30% close × $8k average = **$48k**. Lift close rate to 40% and average sale to $9k and it’s **$72k**. Same leads. No extra ad spend.

Share your numbers, ask what “good” looks like in your industry, and pick one number to improve this month.
