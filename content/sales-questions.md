# ❓ SALES QUESTIONS
General sales questions. No question too basic, as long as it’s real.

**The sort of thing that belongs here:**
• How should I qualify this lead?
• Should I give pricing over the phone?
• How quickly should I follow up?
• Should I offer three options?
• How do I increase my average sale?
• How do I sell premium against cheaper competitors?

**Answering?** Keep it practical. What would you *actually* say or do? If it’s about one specific deal, send them to {#helpMeCloseThis}.
Use a thread for back-and-forth so the channel stays readable.
