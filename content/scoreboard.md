# 📊 THE SCOREBOARD
Every Friday, post your week. Not compulsory, but the members who do this improve fastest. Rough numbers are fine. Honest numbers are required.

```
WEEK:
Leads:
Qualified leads:
Appointments/site visits:
Quotes:
Quotes won:
Revenue won:
Average sale:
Close rate:
Outstanding pipeline:
Biggest win:
Biggest lesson:
Main focus next week:
```

**Quick maths**
• Close rate = quotes won ÷ quotes sent
• Average sale = revenue won ÷ quotes won
• Pipeline = total value of open quotes

Numbers look ugly? Good. Now we know what to fix. Chat about them in {#salesNumbers}.
