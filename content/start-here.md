# 🚦 START HERE
Five steps. Do them this week, not “when things quieten down”. Things never quieten down.

**STEP 1 — Introduce yourself**
Go to {#introductions} and fill out the template. Tell us what you sell and what’s broken.

**STEP 2 — Tell us your numbers**
Roughly how many leads, quotes and sales are you doing a month? Close enough is good enough. You can’t fix what you don’t measure. Post it weekly in {#scoreboard}.

**STEP 3 — Learn the framework**
Read {#theFramework}. Ten minutes. Intrigue → Pain → Empathy → Antidote → Action. It’s the backbone of everything we coach.

**STEP 4 — Bring us a real deal**
Don’t just lurk and consume. Post a live opportunity in {#helpMeCloseThis}, an objection in {#objectionClinic}, or a job you lost in {#lostDeals}. That’s where the value is.

**STEP 5 — Attend the Sales Clinic**
Live group coaching every week. Check {#coachingCalendar} for the next one, drop your question in {#submitAQuestion}, and bring a real situation.
